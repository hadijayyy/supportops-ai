---
document_id: POL-LST-020
title: Lost Package Resolution
version: 2.0
category: lost_packages
effective_date: 2026-04-05
---

A package is considered lost when the carrier marks it lost, or when delivery is at least 5 days late and tracking has had no movement for 72 hours. Eligible lost orders may be refunded or replaced, subject to refund authorization controls.
